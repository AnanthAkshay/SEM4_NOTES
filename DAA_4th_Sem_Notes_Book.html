Course Syllabus Blueprint

Unit 1: Foundations & Recurrences
Algorithmic design steps, Asymptotic notations (O, Θ, Ω), recursive and non-recursive analysis, Tower of Hanoi recurrence, substitution method, recursion tree, Master Theorem, and probabilistic analysis.
Unit 2: Sorting, Medians & Pattern Matching
Binary heap structures, HeapSort, Priority Queues, QuickSort tracing and tree of calls, Counting Sort, Radix Sort, Bucket Sort, Quick-Select, Median finding, and Horspool pattern matching.
Unit 3: Amortized Analysis & Advanced Heaps
Amortized techniques (Aggregate, Accounting, Potential methods), Disjoint set union-find (Union by Rank & Path Compression), and Fibonacci Heaps structure, operations and graph applications.
Unit 4: Dynamic Programming & Greedy Algorithms
Dynamic programming fundamentals, Matrix Chain Multiplication, Longest Common Subsequence, Optimal BST. Greedy techniques, Activity selection, Huffman coding, Kruskal and Prim MSTs, and Dijkstra's algorithm.
Unit 5: Graph Flows, Complexities & Shortest Paths
BFS & DFS traversals, Topological sort, Strongly Connected Components (Kosaraju's), Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall, Johnson's algorithm, Maximum flow (Ford-Fulkerson & Edmonds-Karp), P vs NP classes, and approximation algorithms.
