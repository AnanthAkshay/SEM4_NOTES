Formula: \( P(X > a) = \int_{x=a}^{1} \int_{y=0}^{2} f(x,y) \,dy\,dx \), \( P(Y < X) = \int_{x=0}^{1} \int_{y=0}^{x} f(x,y) \,dy\,dx \).

\[ \begin{aligned} P(X > 1/2) &= \int_{1/2}^{1} \int_{0}^{2} \frac{x^2+xy}{3} \,dy\,dx \\ &= \int_{1/2}^{1} \frac{1}{3}\left[ \int_{0}^{2} (x^2+xy)\,dy \right] dx \\ &= \int_{1/2}^{1} \frac{1}{3} \left[ x^2 y + \frac{x y^2}{2} \right]_{0}^{2} dx \\ &= \int_{1/2}^{1} \frac{1}{3} (2x^2 + 2x) dx = \frac{2}{3} \int_{1/2}^{1} (x^2 + x) dx \\ &= \frac{2}{3} \left[ \frac{x^3}{3} + \frac{x^2}{2} \right]_{1/2}^{1} \\ &= \frac{2}{3} \left[ \left(\frac13+\frac12\right) - \left(\frac{1}{24}+\frac{1}{8}\right) \right] \\ &= \frac{2}{3} \left( \frac{5}{6} - \frac{1}{6} \right) = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{4}{6} = \frac{8}{18} = \frac{4}{9} \approx 0.4444 \end{aligned} \] (ii) P(Y < X):
