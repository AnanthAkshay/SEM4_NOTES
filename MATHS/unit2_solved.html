📊 GAMMA DISTRIBUTION

29. PDF of Gamma(α,β)
\(f(x)=\frac{1}{\beta^\alpha \Gamma(\alpha)} x^{\alpha-1}e^{-x/\beta},\ x>0\) (scale form).
30. Mean and S.D. of Gamma
\(\mu=\alpha\beta,\ \sigma=\sqrt{\alpha}\beta\).
